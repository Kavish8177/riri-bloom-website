OUR STORY

Small creations, made with a big heart.

Welcome to Riri Bloom, a small crochet business based in New Zealand, where creativity and handmade beauty come together.

We believe handmade creations have a special charm. Every stitch takes patience, care and attention to detail, turning simple yarn into something meaningful.

From colourful crochet flowers and cute keychains to charming accessories and plushies, our collection is all about creating little things that bring happiness.

Whether you are looking for a thoughtful gift, something special for yourself or a personalised design, we would love to help bring your idea to life.
