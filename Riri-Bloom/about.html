MADE WITH HEART

Every stitch tells a story.

Welcome to Riri Bloom, a small crochet business based in New Zealand, where creativity and handmade beauty come together.

We believe that handmade creations have a special charm. Every stitch takes patience, care and attention to detail, turning simple yarn into something meaningful.

From colourful crochet flowers and cute keychains to charming accessories and plushies, our collection is all about creating little things that bring happiness.

Whether you are looking for a thoughtful gift, something special for yourself, or a personalised design, we would love to help make your idea come to life.
